Your privacy

WebCatch processes AI detection directly in your browser whenever possible. Your live camera feed is not automatically uploaded or recorded. Detection history and your name are saved only in this browser's local storage — clearing it removes everything.

About WebCatch

WebCatch is a real-time AI vision experience that combines camera technology, hand tracking, gesture recognition, and object detection directly in an interactive web environment. Detection quality depends on lighting, your device, and the underlying models below.
